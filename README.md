## About

MuE-Air is a private, ad-free music app for Android. It streams music, plays your local files,
keeps your library in sync, and lets you listen together with friends in real time — in an app
that weighs under 10 MB.

**Free forever. No ads. No trackers. No subscriptions.**

---

## Features

| **Music**                                                                         | **Listen Together**                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Stream up to 320 kbps from a direct source, with automatic fallback               | Host a room and share a six-character code          |
| Content quality filter blocks AI-generated tracks, karaoke, bootlegs and bad rips | Join from the app with a code — no account required |
| Smart shuffle with artist spacing and recent-history penalty                      | Up to 20 listeners in sync                          |
| Time-synced lyrics with offline cache                                             | DJ mode (host controls) or collaborative mode       |

## Guest mode

Open the app and start listening or join a room instantly — no sign-up, no email, no phone number.
A guest gets a private session with the same player, search, library and Listen Together features.

Sign in only if you want your library, likes and history on more than one device.

## Requirements

- Android 10 (API 29) or newer
- arm64-v8a or armeabi-v7a
- About 10 MB of storage
- No Google Play Services required

## Privacy

- No ads, no subscriptions.
- No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
- Guest mode sends no personal data.
- Chat and social features do not exist in this app — there is nothing to follow, message or share.
- You can delete your account and cloud data at any time from Profile.
